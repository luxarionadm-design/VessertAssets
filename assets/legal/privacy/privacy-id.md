# VESSERTID GLOBAL PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION POLICY
*Document Version: 4.0.0-PROD | Effective Date: October 10, 2026*

---

### 1. DATA CONTROLLER COMMITMENT
**VessertID Digital Ecosystem Inc.** ("VessertID", "We", "Us") acts as the Data Controller for personal data collected, processed, and maintained through the VessertID 3D spatial rendering platform, web applications, and mobile apps. We adhere to international security and privacy frameworks including ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA.

---

### 2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA PROCESSED
1. **Special & Biometric Data**:
   - Facial mesh coordinates are processed **strictly on-device within GPU local memory** for real-time AR filter tracking. Biometric coordinate vectors are NEVER transmitted or stored on VessertID servers.
2. **Account & Identity Data**:
   - Full name, verified business email, handle (@username), avatar image, and verified phone number.
3. **Technical & Telemetry Data**:
   - Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser version, GPU vendor & renderer strings, display resolution, crash logs, and render latency metrics.
4. **Financial Data**:
   - Transaction records and encrypted payment tokens (processed by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways; no raw credit card numbers are stored).

---

### 3. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING
We process personal data based on:
1. **Explicit Consent**: For account registration, media uploads, and camera/microphone permissions.
2. **Contractual Performance**: To deliver spatial cloud rendering, asset storage, and subscription processing.
3. **Compliance with Legal Obligations**: For tax compliance and statutory incident reporting.
4. **Legitimate Interests**: To protect systems against DDoS attacks, fraud, and vulnerability exploits.

---

### 4. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
You have the right to:
- Access, rectify, or download your personal data in portable JSON/CSV format.
- Request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") of your account and 3D assets.
- Withdraw processing consent at any time via Account Privacy Settings.
- Request human review of automated content moderation decisions.

---

### 5. SECURITY & ENCRYPTION
- **In Transit**: All network communication is encrypted using TLS 1.3 with high-strength cipher suites.
- **At Rest**: Object storage, database volumes, and encrypted backups are secured using AES-256.
